# VIVA+ User Meeting 2024

Welcome to the online VIVA+ User Meeting on January 30, 2024

:simple-youtube: [Recording on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zDQkC370mcg)

<!-- more -->

Whether you are a VIVA+ user or never used it before but curious to know more, join us online to hear more from the VIVA+ User community!

### What to expect?

- new __VIVA+ validations__ (and how you could reproduce these on your own)
- new features in __VIVA+ v2.0.0__
- __use cases__ from the VIVA+ User community
- __OpenRadioss__ portability (for the complete [Open Source](../../about/open-science.md) workflow)
- and much more
